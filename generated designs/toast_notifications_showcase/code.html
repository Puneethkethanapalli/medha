The observer effect in quantum mechanics implies that the act of measurement affects the system being observed. How can I elaborate on this further?
Give me a brief summary of the double-slit experiment to illustrate that.
smart_toy Medha AI
In the double-slit experiment, particles like electrons are fired at a screen with two slits. When unobserved, they create an interference pattern, behaving like waves. When measured at the slits, they act like particles, and the interference pattern disappears. This demonstrates wave-particle duality and the observer effect.
